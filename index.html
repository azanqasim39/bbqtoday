Frequently Asked

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Where exactly is BBQ Today located in Grand Mall? ▾

We are conveniently situated inside Grand Mall, Al Rashidiya 3, Ajman (Plus code: 9XVR+928). There is ample mall parking and quick curb access for curbside collection.

How does Curbside Pickup work? ▾

Simply click the "Call" or "WhatsApp" button to send us your order 15 minutes before arrival. When you pull up outside Grand Mall entrance, let us know your car model/color and our staff will deliver your hot order directly to your window!

Do you deliver across Ajman and nearby areas? ▾

Yes! We offer direct hot delivery across Ajman. Send your location pin and order on WhatsApp to +971 52 285 4003 for instant dispatch.

Is all meat 100% Halal? ▾

Yes, 100%. All meats, poultry, cheese, and ingredients are certified Halal and sourced fresh daily from verified UAE suppliers.
